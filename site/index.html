Path Patrol v1.0.0

How to play

  1. Start on an edge. Touch the frame or any ground you have already claimed.
  2. Draw across the field in any shape. The route appears under your finger the moment you move.
  3. Finish on an edge. The side without patrols is yours. Claim the target percentage to win the level.
  4. Don't get caught. If a patrol touches a route you are still drawing, you lose a life. Lifting mid-field cancels it for free.
